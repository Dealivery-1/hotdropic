Shipping & returns

Last updated November 18, 2026

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States.

How long it takes

  • Meme tees and hoodies are printed just for you. Printing takes about 2–5 business days, then shipping takes about 3–5 business days.
  • Everything else ships from our partner warehouses. Each product page shows its delivery estimate (usually 4–15 business days).
  • If your order has items from different makers, they can arrive in separate packages.

Every order gets tracking. You can check it any time on the order page with your order number and email.

What shipping costs

Shipping is free on orders over the amount shown at the top of the store (currently $35). Below that, a flat shipping fee is shown in your cart and at checkout before you pay.

If we're running late

If we can't ship your order within the time shown when you ordered, we'll email you with a new estimate, and you can cancel for a full refund.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

Email wolfmaddox07@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or give you a full refund. You don't need to send it back.

Lost packages

If tracking hasn't moved for 10 days, or it says delivered but you can't find it, email us. We'll work with the carrier and replace it or refund you.

Returns for other reasons

  • Printed apparel is made to order, so we can't take it back for a change of mind or the wrong size. Check the size notes on the product page before you order.
  • Other items: email us within 30 days of delivery. If the item is unused and in its original packaging, we'll send return instructions and refund the item price once it arrives (return shipping is on you). For some low-cost items we may simply refund you without a return.

Refunds go back to your original payment method. Banks usually show them within 5–10 business days.

Cancelling

Orders go to production quickly. Email us within 2 hours of ordering to cancel. After that, we'll cancel if the item hasn't started production or shipped yet.
