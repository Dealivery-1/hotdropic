Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what The Hot Dropic (run by Maddox Wolf, Missouri, USA) collects and why.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email, phone number, shipping address and what you bought. Stripe collects your payment details directly; we never see your full card number.
  • When you browse: your cart is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it's there when you come back. Our host, Cloudflare, keeps standard technical logs (like IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running. We don't use advertising trackers.

How we use it

To make and ship your order, give you tracking, answer your questions, handle returns and refunds, prevent fraud, and keep records the law requires.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the store, and only what they need: Stripe (payments), Printful (printing and shipping apparel), CJ Dropshipping (other products), the shipping carriers they use, Cloudflare (hosting), and, if we email you about shipping, our email provider. We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context advertising.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for taxes, accounting and returns (usually up to 7 years). You can ask us to delete anything we don't need to keep.

Your choices

Email wolfmaddox07@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your information. We'll answer within 30 days. You can clear your saved cart any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Kids

The store isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.
